Independent seats are never one party — each is a different local candidate with their own personal following, grouped into a single block per ward only so the count can run, and they keep their own slider above. Every other small party — the Scottish Family Party, the British Unionist Party, TUSC, and the rest — is tracked individually in the count using its own real local 2022 result, ward by ward, so if one wins a seat above it's shown by its real name. They share a single combined "Other" figure rather than one slider each: it isn't adjustable directly, but is whatever's left once you've set the six main parties, and scales every minor party up or down together while keeping their relative local strength fixed.
