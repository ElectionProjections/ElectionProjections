Spain Seat Projection Model

Set a national vote share for each party and this applies a proportional national swing to the actual result in each of Spain's 52 constituencies from the 23 July 2023 general election, then re-runs the D'Hondt allocation, seat by seat, exactly as the Junta Electoral does.

Parties

Enter the national vote share you want to test for each party. Every party's constituency-level 2023 result is scaled by (your figure ÷ its 2023 national share), so doubling a party's national share doubles it in every seat it contests — and setting it to 0% removes it everywhere. Regional and nationalist parties are shown only where they actually ran in 2023.
