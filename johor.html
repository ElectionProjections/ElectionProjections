Seat-by-seat breakdown ▾

Methodology: Universal national swing (UNS) from official 2026 Johor state election results (11 Jul 2026 polling day; source: Election Commission of Malaysia via BERNAMA) applied to BN, PH, PN, BP, and BERSAMA. BP comprises MUDA and PSM; BERSAMA is included as its own baseline for the first time, reflecting its real 2026 seat-level vote share rather than a folded-in estimate. Independents and small parties (BEBAS, ASLI) are redistributed proportionally among contesting parties before swing, weighted by their raw 2026 seat-level shares — no minor candidate can win a seat in the projection. Taper scalar applied below 50% of each party's 2026 share. PN's contest footprint is carried forward unchanged from 2026 as a placeholder assumption, pending confirmed candidate lists; PN non-contest seats have PN's projected share redistributed — 66% to BN, 33% to PH. BP and BERSAMA are assumed to contest all 56 seats at the next election, even though each only fielded candidates in a subset in 2026 (BP: 5 seats; BERSAMA: 15) — their 2026 baseline vote share is correctly 0 in the seats they sat out, so this assumption only affects which seats can now show a projected BP/BERSAMA share, not the historical baseline itself. Majority: 29 of 56 seats.
