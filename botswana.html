Method: Universal National Swing. Each party's entered vote share is compared with its 2024 national result to give a swing in percentage points; that swing is then applied to the same party's actual vote share in every individual constituency, renormalised, and whichever party finishes highest wins the seat. Map, seat bar, party cards and tooltips are all counted from that single projection, so they always agree. Each hex is one tile of a shared lattice and each constituency is a cluster of tiles, sized by the share of the country nearest to it and placed at its true geographic position, so the tiles tessellate with no gaps. The national border behind is real boundary data. 58 of the 61 constituencies list every candidate who stood, from each seat's published results table or the Botswana Daily News constituency declarations. The other three (Lentsweletau-Lephephe, Takatokwane, Letlhakeng) have no full candidate breakdown published, so those tooltips show the winner and runner-up and state that the rest isn't available.
