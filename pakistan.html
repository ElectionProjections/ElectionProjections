National Assembly Seat Projection

Uniform National Swing applied to the real result in all 266 NA constituencies from GE‑2024, with a taper below a fifth of each party's 2024 support so a party dialled toward zero heads to zero everywhere — including its own strongholds. The vote pool left over by the nine largest parties is split proportionally across the real minor parties and independents who actually won seats in 2024, each with the same taper.
